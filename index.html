Terms & Conditions

All participants and contestants intending to participate in the Tai Game Festival are required to read, understand, and agree to these terms before completing registration.

1. ELIGIBILITY

  • Participation is open to individuals who meet the eligibility requirements of their chosen category.
  • All contestants must provide accurate and verifiable personal information during registration.
  • Participants must comply with the rules and regulations governing their respective competitions.
  • The organisers reserve the right to disqualify any participant who provides false information or violates the festival's rules.

2. CATEGORY B – PAGEANTRY (RESTRICTION)

  • Participation in Category B (Pageantry) is strictly limited to indigenes of Tai Local Government Area, Rivers State.
  • Non-indigenes of Tai Local Government Area are not eligible to register or contest in any of the pageantry competitions.
  • Contestants may be required to provide valid proof of Tai indigene status when requested by the organisers.
  • The organisers reserve the right to verify the indigene status of all pageantry contestants.

3. REGISTRATION AND PAYMENT

  • All registrations must be completed through the appropriate authorities or officially designated registration channels recognised by the Tai Game Festival organisers.
  • Contestants are responsible for ensuring that their registration details are correctly submitted.
  • Registration fees must be paid through the officially approved payment channels or as directed by the appropriate authorities.
  • Registration is only considered valid upon confirmation by the designated registration authority.

4. NO REFUND POLICY

  • All registration fees paid are strictly non-refundable.
  • No refund shall be entertained after payment has been made, regardless of whether a contestant withdraws, becomes unavailable, is disqualified, or decides not to participate.
  • Contestants are therefore advised to carefully consider their participation and confirm their eligibility before making payment.

5. CONDUCT AND SPORTSMANSHIP

  • All participants are expected to demonstrate discipline, respect, fairness, and good sportsmanship throughout the festival.
  • Fighting, intimidation, harassment, cheating, manipulation of results, or any form of unsportsmanlike behaviour will not be tolerated.
  • Participants must respect fellow contestants, officials, organisers, and spectators.
  • Any participant found guilty of misconduct may face disqualification.

6. RULES AND REGULATIONS

  • Each competition or game shall operate under its specific rules and guidelines as established by the organisers.
  • Participants are expected to familiarise themselves with the rules of their chosen competition before participating.
  • The organisers reserve the right to make necessary adjustments to competition schedules, formats, or procedures where circumstances demand.

7. DISQUALIFICATION

The organisers reserve the right to disqualify any contestant who:

  • Provides false or misleading registration information.
  • Violates the rules and regulations of the festival.
  • Engages in cheating, manipulation, or fraudulent activities.
  • Displays conduct capable of disrupting the peace and integrity of the festival.
  • Fails to meet the eligibility requirements of their chosen category.

8. DECISIONS OF THE ORGANISERS

  • Decisions made by authorised competition officials and judges shall be respected.
  • Where necessary, the organisers shall establish appropriate procedures for addressing complaints or disputes.
  • The organisers reserve the right to interpret and enforce these terms and conditions in the overall interest of fairness, transparency, and the successful conduct of the festival.

9. ACCEPTANCE OF TERMS

By registering for the Tai Game Festival and making payment where applicable, every participant or contestant confirms that they have read, understood, and agreed to abide by these Terms and Conditions.

IMPORTANT NOTICE: Registration for Category B (Pageantry) is exclusively reserved for indigenes of Tai Local Government Area. All registration processes must be handled through the appropriate authorities, and all payments made are non-refundable.
